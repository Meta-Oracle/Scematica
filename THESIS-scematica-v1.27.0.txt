SCEMATICA v1.27.0 -- A DISSERTATION ON THE REPRESENTATION OF IGNORANCE IN AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS
Fourteen versions of treating "unmeasured" as a value distinct from zero, across seven subsystems, with an accounting of what the discipline cost and where it remains unfalsified
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A previous dissertation on this system (v1.13.0) diagnosed a production trading bot that had stopped trading, and found the cause was not threshold strictness but four input signals that were structurally constant at zero. It closed with a diagnostic rule: before adjusting any threshold, verify that the quantity it compares against is capable of varying. This dissertation reports what happened when that rule was promoted from a diagnostic procedure to a design constraint and applied across fourteen subsequent versions and seven newly built subsystems.
The central claim is a generalisation of the earlier finding. The defect in v1.13.0 was not that four particular fields were dead. It was that the system had no representation for "this quantity was not measured" distinct from its representation for "this quantity was measured and found to be zero", and so every layer above the measurement -- the gates, the score, the learned policy, the log, and finally the operator's own diagnosis -- inherited a false observation that was indistinguishable from a true one. Zero is the attractor for unrepresented ignorance because it is the additive identity, the default initialiser, the value a missing JSON field deserialises into, and the number that makes an equation typecheck. Every one of those is a reason to reach for it, and none of them is a measurement.
Nine findings are reported. The arithmetic of the neutral element, where a multiplicative coherence index was pinned permanently at zero by a single unmeasured input, twice, in two independent implementations built eight versions apart. Provenance carried ahead of value, so that a score cannot be read without its coverage. Rendering as the last enforcement surface, where a rule about trust is given exactly one implementation because a copy that drifts is worse than no copy. Abstention as a first-class outcome with five distinguishable reasons and an exit code of zero. An epistemic circuit breaker that halts trading on the condition preceding loss rather than on loss. Counterfactual machinery that counts what it cannot score. A wire contract carrying ignorance across four language boundaries, validated twice on each side. The same discipline applied to natural language, where the final layer is prose written by a model that cannot be constrained by a type system. And the same discipline applied to custody, where "could not read the reserve" and "the reserve is zero" are different claims and only one of them is an accusation.
The dissertation reports its negative results at equal length. A blockchain port was built, measured, and found to produce approximately two qualifying events in eight days -- a rate that does not support the product it was built for, a result recorded rather than buried. The majority of the machinery described here is not on the trading path and has therefore not been falsified by money. And the discipline has a measurable cost, quantified in Section 13.3: an agent that can say "I do not know" says it often, and on a barely-perceived world it abstains from almost everything.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Motivation
The failure mode this dissertation is about does not announce itself. A system that has confused ignorance with zero does not crash, does not log an error, and does not produce a number of the wrong shape. It produces a number of exactly the right shape, in the right units, within the expected range, that happens to be a fabrication. Everything downstream then works perfectly on it. This is the characteristic that makes the defect class expensive: the correctness of every layer above the measurement is preserved, so debugging effort concentrates there and finds nothing wrong, because nothing there is wrong.
The earlier dissertation demonstrated one instance of this at the level of a single trading pipeline. What was not clear at the time, and what fourteen versions of building have since made unmissable, is that the same defect reappears at every scale and in every medium: in a floating-point product, in a JSON field, in a terminal glyph, in an SVG arc, in an exit code, in a sentence written by a language model, and in the balance of a token account holding somebody's money. It reappears because the pressure that produces it is not domain-specific. It is the pressure to make an expression evaluate.
1.2 Prior work in this system
Version 1.13.0 documented five findings in a live sniper: four structurally dead signals, a momentum gate vetoing on a measurement its own sampling rate could not support, a Fibonacci gate rejecting 198 candidates by a floating-point margin of 6e-17, a Double-DQN agent that had collapsed onto a single action while presenting high apparent conviction, and an infrastructure layer causing safety filters to fail open. Post-intervention acceptance moved from 1.19% to 16.0%. Its concluding principle -- Section 12.4 of that document -- was a diagnostic one, addressed to an investigator holding a decision log.
That principle contained three corollaries, and the second is the seed of everything reported here: missing data is not bad data. Imputing a penalty for an unmeasurable quantity applies a uniform tax that shifts the score distribution without discriminating within it. Stated that way it is a remark about scoring. Stated more generally it is a claim about representation, and that is the claim this dissertation tests.
1.3 Thesis statement
Three claims are advanced.
First, that "unmeasured" and "measured as zero" are distinct states which must be distinguishable at every layer of a system, from the arithmetic through the wire format to the rendered glyph, and that a system which collapses them will produce confident false outputs at a rate its own tests cannot detect, because the collapse happens before the tests observe anything.
Second, that the discipline required is not primarily arithmetic but architectural: it is cheap to define a type that carries a measured flag and expensive to keep every consumer of that type honest, and the places where the honesty is actually lost are the aggregation, the serialisation boundary, and the renderer -- in that order of frequency and inverse order of attention.
Third, that a system built this way must be able to abstain, that abstention must carry a reason specific enough to act on, and that abstention must not be encoded as failure -- because a caller that learns to treat "the system declined" as a crash will be rewritten to ignore the signal, and will then ignore real crashes too.
1.4 Contributions
The contributions are: a documented instance of the same multiplicative-zero defect occurring twice, independently, eight versions apart, in two implementations written by parties who had each read the other's post-mortem; a type-level mechanism (Provenance, Term, Coverage) that carries ignorance through aggregation, with the additive utility form that mechanism forces; an enumeration of the rendering failures that defeat a correct model, including two that are specific to vector graphics and one specific to colour; a five-way taxonomy of abstention with the operator instruction each implies; an epistemic circuit breaker that fires on unverified inputs rather than on realised loss, with its instrumentation sited so that new consumers are covered by construction; a wire contract carrying the same distinctions across four languages, with the fixed-point commitment scheme that made it verifiable; the application of all of the above to a language model's output, which is the only layer with no type system at all; and a negative result in which a measurement retired a product.
1.5 What this dissertation does not claim
It does not claim a profitability improvement. No new live trading measurement is presented; the performance figures cited in Section 13.2 are those of the v1.13.0 window and are reproduced as context, not as a result. It does not claim the discipline is novel -- option types, null-safety and missing-data handling are old ideas, and the contribution here is an account of where they fail in practice under sustained pressure, not an announcement that they exist. And it does not claim the system described is correct. It claims the system is now capable of saying which of its numbers it does not stand behind, which is a strictly weaker and considerably more useful property.

2. THE DEFECT CLASS, GENERALISED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 From dead signals to unrepresented ignorance
The v1.13.0 findings can be restated without reference to trading. In each case a consumer required a value; the producer had no value to give; and the interface between them could express only a value. The producer therefore supplied zero. Because zero is a legal value of the type, no error was raised, no branch was taken, and no observer downstream could recover the fact that a substitution had occurred. The gate then evaluated a predicate over a constant and reported a decision that had the form of a judgement and the content of a coin with one face.
Generalised: the defect is a lossy conversion at an interface that has no channel for the loss. It is not a bug in the producer, which had nothing to report, and it is not a bug in the consumer, which correctly evaluated what it was handed. It is a bug in the interface, and interfaces are exactly what tests are worst at covering, because a test written against the interface uses the same impoverished vocabulary the interface has.
2.2 The three encodings of "I do not know"
Three encodings appear across the system studied, and they are not interchangeable.
The first is absence: the field is not present. This is the honest encoding and the least convenient, because every consumer must handle it, and a language that permits the field to be missing without forcing that handling converts absence into the second encoding silently.
The second is a sentinel: a value inside the type's range, reserved by convention to mean "no value". Zero, -1, empty string, epoch. Every sentinel is a lie waiting for the day the reserved value becomes a legitimate measurement, and in this system that day arrived for zero more than once, because a pool really can have zero volume and a coverage really can be zero.
The third is a companion flag: the value plus a boolean saying whether it means anything. This is what the system converged on -- `Term { value, measured }` in the mesh, `Provenance` in the runtime, `heartbeatMeasured` in the oracle toolkit, `activity: null` versus `activity: 0.0` on a mesh node. It is verbose and it is the only one of the three that survives aggregation, which is the property that matters, because aggregation is where the information is actually lost.
2.3 Why zero is the attractor
Zero is not chosen; it accumulates. It is the additive identity, so a sum over an unmeasured term is unchanged and the code looks right. It is the default for a numeric field in most serialisation libraries, so an absent JSON key becomes a real zero with no diagnostic. It is what an uninitialised accumulator holds. It is the value that makes a multiplication typecheck and a division not divide. And critically, it is the answer that lets a function keep its signature: a function returning `f64` cannot decline, so the author, needing to return something, returns the number that feels least like a claim. It feels least like a claim and it is the strongest possible claim the type can make.

3. METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 The corpus
The evidence is the system itself across versions 1.13.0 to 1.27.0: the bot workspace (sniper, arbitrage, executor, the learning agent, the risk breakers, the sentience library, the mesh collector), the agent runtime published separately as Scematica Omni, a stdlib-only Python oracle toolkit, an EVM chain port, seven web surfaces, two on-chain programs, a browser extension, and a conversational terminal over all of it. Each subsystem is treated as an independent application of the same design constraint, which is what makes the sample interesting: they were built at different times, in four languages, by parties with different amounts of the earlier post-mortem in front of them.
3.2 What counts as evidence for a design claim
A design claim cannot be established by a benchmark. What is available instead is a specific and unglamorous form of evidence: the record of what went wrong when the claim was violated. Every finding below is anchored to a defect that was actually hit, in this system, and in most cases the defect was hit *after* the principle had been written down, by the same people who wrote it down. That is the strongest available evidence that the principle is not obvious, because obvious principles are not violated by their own authors.
Where a finding rests on a test rather than an incident, this is stated. Where it rests on neither -- where it is a design decision whose consequences have not yet been observed -- it appears in Section 13.2 among the unfalsified, not among the findings.
3.3 The falsification standard
The thesis would be falsified by either of two observations. First, a case where carrying the measured flag through aggregation produced no behavioural difference from a well-chosen sentinel -- that is, where the ceremony bought nothing. Second, a case where a system that could abstain abstained so often that its output was less useful than a system that guessed. The second is not hypothetical, and it is reported in Section 13.3 as a partial confirmation of the risk rather than as a refutation, for reasons given there.

4. FINDING I: THE ARITHMETIC OF THE NEUTRAL ELEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 The multiplicative gate and its zero
A coherence or integrity index built as a product of normalised sub-scores has an attractive property: it is unforgiving. No single dimension can be compensated by another, so a system with excellent reasoning and broken perception scores low, which is correct and is precisely why the form was chosen. The trading equations in this project use the same shape deliberately, placing a capture coefficient in front of an edge as a multiplier so that a broken executor cannot be offset by better selection.
The property that makes it unforgiving is also what makes it fatal to unrepresented ignorance. A product is zero if any factor is zero. If an unmeasured dimension is encoded as zero, the index is not merely degraded, it is annihilated, and it is annihilated in a way that cannot be distinguished from total measured failure.
4.2 The sentience index pinned at zero
The first occurrence was in the sentience library's perception term, where the data ratio is a product over input channels. A single channel that nobody had instrumented scored zero, which pinned the whole index at zero permanently. The gate above it, which returns GO, CAUTION or HOLD, therefore returned HOLD forever, on a system that was entirely healthy. The correction is that an unmeasured dimension takes the value 1.0 -- read as "not a limiting factor" -- and only measured degradation moves the verdict.
The second-order failure is worth stating because it is the one that destroys the feature rather than the number. A gate that sits in CAUTION on a healthy system trains its operators to ignore it within about a week, after which the gate is decorative. An always-on warning and an absent warning convey the same information, and the always-on one costs screen space and credibility.
4.3 The agentic gate pinned shut on unbuilt subsystems
The second occurrence was in the mesh's cognition module, eight versions later, implementing a specification whose literal reading assigns zero to any term whose subsystem does not exist. Since the specification describes subsystems nobody had built, the literal reading pins the gate shut on the absence of future work. The identical correction was applied: every term carries `measured: bool`, and an unmeasured dimension contributes the neutral element for its operator -- 1.0 under multiplication, 0.0 under addition -- never a substituted zero under multiplication.
That this happened twice, in one project, with the first incident documented, is the single most persuasive datum in this dissertation. The authors of the second implementation had read the first post-mortem. The pull toward the annihilating encoding is not ignorance of the hazard; it is the local reasonableness of writing `0.0` when a value is missing and the expression must evaluate.
4.4 The neutral element rule
Stated generally: when an aggregate must be computed over dimensions that may be unmeasured, the unmeasured dimension must contribute the identity element of the aggregating operation, and the aggregate must carry a count of how many dimensions were measured. The first half preserves the aggregate; the second half is what stops the preserved aggregate from being mistaken for a complete one. Neither half is sufficient alone: an aggregate over one measured term out of nine is a statement about ignorance and must be presented as one, which is why the mesh's `measured_fraction` is never permitted to appear separately from the index it qualifies.
4.5 The additive alternative and why the runtime chose it
The agent runtime's utility function is deliberately additive: `U = R - λ₁K - λ₂C - λ₃U + λ₄V`. A multiplicative form would be more expressive and would encode genuine dependencies between the terms. It was rejected because this project has paid for the multiplicative form twice, and because the additive form degrades gracefully under exactly the condition the runtime is designed for -- a world in which most terms are unmeasured. Under addition, an unmeasured term contributes zero, which is the identity, and the score remains the score of what was actually observed.
The λ weights are a stated preference rather than a fitted parameter, and they are hashed into every sealed decision record. This is a second application of the same discipline at a different level: a fitted weight is a measurement, and presenting a preference as a measurement is the same category error as presenting an absence as a zero.
4.6 A surprising consequence: measuring a term can raise the score
The web gate solver permits an operator to override terms hypothetically and re-evaluate. Overriding an unmeasured term makes it count as measured, which enlarges the denominator of the risk mean, and can therefore *raise* the index. This is counterintuitive, it is correct, and it is pinned by a test -- because the obvious "fix" is to average over all components regardless of measurement, which reports 0.234 for a field whose measured components average 0.351.
The general form of the lesson: once ignorance is represented, the arithmetic over it stops being intuitive, and the intuitive correction is usually a return to the collapsed encoding wearing a different name.

5. FINDING II: PROVENANCE BEFORE VALUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Term, Provenance, Coverage
The runtime's core types make ignorance unrepresentable-as-zero by construction. A `Term` carries a value and a measured flag. A `Provenance` states how a value was obtained -- observed, imported, stale, or absent -- and an `Absent` provenance carries no attributes at all, so there is nothing to accidentally read. A `Coverage` accompanies every aggregate and states how many terms of how many were measured. No renderer is permitted to print a score without its coverage.
The ordering in the name of this section is the design rule: provenance is decided before the value is, and a value whose provenance is `Absent` never acquires one. The failure this prevents is the most common one in perception code, where a walk that could not read a file writes a zero into the count and continues.
5.2 Stale is not fresh and keeps its value
A stale measurement is neither a fresh measurement nor an absent one, and encoding it as either is wrong in a different direction. Encoding it as fresh is a fabrication. Encoding it as absent discards a real observation that remains informative -- an oracle price from twenty minutes ago is not a current price and is also not nothing.
The system therefore carries staleness as its own provenance with the age and the budget attached. A veto from a stale mesh node is not counted as blocking, because it is history rather than an alarm; a feed past its heartbeat is marked stale with its age, not dropped and not presented as current. The oracle toolkit adds a measured refinement: heartbeats are measured per feed per chain rather than assumed from a shared default, and a feed whose heartbeat was never measured is marked `heartbeatMeasured: false` so that a conservative bound is not mistaken for an observation. A staleness tolerance is applied on top, because real publish ceilings run a percent or two over the configured interval, and a feed that flickers stale every cycle trains people to ignore the flag -- the same credibility failure as Section 4.2, arrived at from the opposite direction.
5.3 The unknown denominator
A capped or partial enumeration must report an unknown total rather than a numerator over the smaller total it actually reached. The latter is not merely imprecise; it can claim coverage above 100%, and more often it claims a suspiciously reassuring 100% of a denominator that silently shrank to fit. The runtime's `Extent` carries `total: None` when a cap was hit. This was not theoretical: a self-check in the Python producer caught a real extent defect the first time it ran.
5.4 A deliberate exclusion is not a blind spot
The counterpart rule, and the one most easily got wrong in the enthusiastic direction. An observer that skips a build directory has made a decision, not suffered a failure, and filing that decision as ignorance buries the paths that genuinely could not be read under a list of paths nobody wanted. Over-reporting ignorance destroys the signal exactly as effectively as under-reporting it, and it is harder to notice because it looks like rigour.

6. FINDING III: RENDERING IS AN ENFORCEMENT SURFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 One implementation of the rule
A correct model reaching a careless renderer produces the same output as an incorrect model. The system therefore treats formatting as part of the safety argument: there is exactly one function in the Rust runtime that turns a `Term` into a string, and exactly one function in the web mesh view that maps a state to a colour. Ports exist in the extension, the console and two web surfaces, each independently tested, and the shared artefact is deliberately the *rule* rather than the code -- a copy that drifts is worse than no copy, because a second implementation carries the authority of the first while disagreeing with it.
6.2 The em dash and the measured zero
The rule itself is one line. An unmeasured term renders as an em dash. A measured zero renders as `0.00`, because a measured zero is a real observation and suppressing it would be the mirror-image error. Every surface in the system obeys this, and the plugin skill written for an assistant working on the codebase opens with it, because a configuration file cannot stop a model writing "expected gain: 0.00" when the tool said `—`.
6.3 The same rule in vector form
The instrument ring around the conversational avatar is where this became genuinely difficult, because a vector graphic has no em dash. A zero-length arc is what you get for an index of 0.00 and it is also what you get for "nobody measured the index"; the two are pixel-identical. The resolution is that an unmeasured gauge draws its full sweep dashed and labels itself with an em dash, while a measured zero draws nothing and labels itself `0.00`. Coverage is drawn as one cell per term and never as a proportional bar, because a bar renders two-of-five and four-of-ten identically and the denominator is the number that matters. An empty trace is a flat line and never a plausible squiggle.
Motion is treated as a claim in the same way: the ring is slowest and does not pulse when idle, so that a stopped stream is visible across a room. The general lesson is that every visual channel -- length, fill, colour, motion -- has a default state that reads as a measurement, and each one must be given an explicit non-measurement rendering or it will lie by default.
6.4 Tri-state to the renderer
An edge that could not be read must render differently from an edge that was read and found inactive; a node with no activity measurement must render nothing rather than an empty bar, since an empty bar reads as "measured, and it is zero". This is the same rule as 6.2 applied to shape rather than text, and it is the one most often lost in the final component, where a `null` is coerced to `0` to satisfy a prop type.
6.5 Colour as a claim about trust
Colour is assigned in exactly one place, and provenance outranks verdict everywhere except a live veto. A stale node reporting a pass has not passed anything recently, and painting it the same green as a live pass is precisely the error the feature exists to prevent. The counterpart rule is that colour is decoration and never the message: a test walks every role in monochrome and fails any role that carries neither a modifier nor a distinguishing word, so that a pipe, a monochrome terminal and a colour-blind reader receive the same text.

7. FINDING IV: ABSTENTION AS A FIRST-CLASS OUTCOME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Five reasons, five instructions
The runtime's decision procedure may decline, and it declines with one of five reasons: no candidates, all forbidden, no positive utility, too little measured, contested. These are not severity levels. They are five different instructions to the operator -- go and generate options, go and relax a constraint, go and accept that nothing here is worth doing, go and measure more, go and resolve a disagreement -- and collapsing them into a single "declined" discards the entire actionable content of the outcome.
7.2 Exit code zero
`decide` exits zero when it abstains. The reasoning is behavioural rather than semantic: a script that treats "the agent declined" as a crash gets rewritten by its author to ignore the exit code, and a script that ignores the exit code also ignores real crashes. The choice is therefore between a signal that is respected and a signal that is technically more precise and will be disabled within a month.
7.3 Two correct behaviours that read as faults
Two behaviours were correct at every step and read as malfunctions on first contact. A simulation with no grounding flag abstains, which is right, and looked like the tool refusing to work; what was needed was one flag nobody had heard of. And when a grounded signal branch outranked the operator's own stated goal, the runtime chose the signal branch and said nothing, which looks like success and reads as the goal having been ignored.
Both were rendering defects rather than logic defects, which is the interesting part: the abstention machinery was correct and the abstention was unusable. The repair renders each of the five reasons as a different next command, lists the counted signal identifiers when a goal is ungrounded, and explains a goal that lost. It suggests and never acts -- filling in the grounding flag because it looked plausible would reintroduce the inference defect described in Section 7.4 with a friendlier interface.
7.4 An instruction is not evidence
An earlier version of the hypothesiser inferred grounding by keyword overlap between the goal text and the observed signals. It immediately grounded a goal about adding tests to one crate in a marker backlog belonging to a different crate, because the project's name is a substring of every unit name in it. Grounding is now asserted by the operator and never inferred, and the console has a test pinning that a goal naming a signal identifier verbatim still does not ground it.
The general statement is that a stated intention is not an observation. A system that treats the operator's phrasing as evidence has manufactured a measurement out of a request, which is the defect of this dissertation running in the opposite direction: not ignorance encoded as data, but data conjured from a wish.
7.5 The specialist that declines in two ways
A domain specialist -- here, the trading agent evaluating a non-trading world -- may decline in two distinguishable ways: permanently out of domain, or in-domain but missing inputs. Only the second is actionable, and merging them would tell an operator that a fixable gap is a permanent limitation. The specialist also refuses a partial or stale input rather than defaulting a missing feature to zero, which the network would otherwise read as a genuine observation of an empty pool.
Specialist scores are attached to a ranking and never averaged into it, because a utility and a normalised Q-value are not the same quantity. A qualified specialist's measured negative vetoes outright; an unmeasured one is silence and carries no veto. That asymmetry is the whole finding in one sentence.

8. FINDING V: THE EPISTEMIC BREAKER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Breakers that fire on money and breakers that fire on evidence
The system carries several independent circuit breakers: a drawdown tracker, a five-minute cumulative-loss window, fractional Kelly sizing, a deployer reputation ledger. Every one of them fires on money, and therefore after the damage. The coherence breaker is the only one that fires on the condition that precedes it.
8.2 Fail-open as a pipeline-wide property
The filter pipeline's RPC-bound checks fail open on timeout, by necessity: a queue that stalls on one slow node is worse than a queue that lets a candidate through. The consequence, which is invisible at any single site, is that a degraded node converts the pipeline into a pass-through that still reports "passed". Past some fraction of unresolved checks, the safety filters the operator believes are running are silently not running, and the log is indistinguishable from a healthy one.
This is the v1.13.0 fail-open hazard elevated from a per-filter concern to a property of the pipeline as a whole. It is also the clearest example of the defect class costing money rather than opportunity, since the earlier findings mostly cost trades not taken.
8.3 Instrumentation at the retry helper, not the site
The counter lives in the two shared RPC retry helpers rather than at each fail-open call site. This is a deliberate choice about future correctness: a new filter added next year is counted by construction, whereas per-site instrumentation is correct exactly until someone forgets. The index is computed by the same library and the same equation as the API's gate, so there is one definition rather than two -- the same reasoning as the single renderer in Section 6.1.
8.4 Buys only
The breaker halts buys and never sells. A degraded feed must never prevent an operator from closing existing risk. This is a general asymmetry worth stating: a safety mechanism that reduces exposure under uncertainty should be permitted to block the action that increases exposure and forbidden from blocking the action that decreases it, and the two are not symmetric merely because both are trades.
8.5 The minimum sample
The breaker cannot trip before a minimum number of observations, so it cannot fire at startup, when it knows least. A safety mechanism whose confidence is lowest exactly when its willingness to act is highest will fire spuriously on its first run, and a spurious first firing is how a breaker gets disabled in configuration and never re-enabled. Relatedly, the configuration flag defaults on through an explicit default function, because a bare optional boolean deserialises to false and would have silently disabled the feature for every configuration file already in existence -- a serialisation default quietly overriding a safety decision, which is Section 2.3's attractor operating on a boolean instead of a float.

9. FINDING VI: COUNTERFACTUALS THAT REFUSE TO BE SCORED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 The replay asymmetry
Counterfactual replay re-applies thresholds to what the pipeline actually measured. Tightening a threshold yields an exact profit-and-loss delta, because the excluded candidates were really traded and their outcomes are known. Loosening a threshold admits candidates nobody bought, for which no outcome exists -- and none is estimated. The two directions of the same operation return answers of different epistemic kinds, and the interface says so rather than smoothing them into one number.
This asymmetry is uncomfortable, because the loosening direction is the one an operator most wants answered, and it is exactly the direction that cannot be answered. Presenting an estimate there would be the most damaging possible application of the defect class: a fabricated return, generated by the system, attached to a decision the system is recommending.
9.2 Calibration and the unresolved claim
The conversational assistant's past claims are scored the same way. Bullish calls resolve against realised profit and loss. Bearish calls almost never resolve, because the bot avoided those pools and no outcome was ever generated. Unresolved claims are counted, not scored, and the mean absolute error is null rather than zero when nothing has resolved.
The same rule holds in the runtime, where every declined branch is remembered together with what was projected for it, and its realised outcome almost never exists because nobody ran it. Imputing outcomes for untaken branches would mean a system generating its own training signal, which is the failure mode that terminates in a model confidently reporting the world it assumed.
9.3 Scoping a claim to a sentence
A claim is scoped to the sentence naming the subject, never to the whole message. A paragraph mentioning four assets would otherwise manufacture four opinions that were never held, and the calibration record -- the one artefact the assistant does not control -- would fill with fabricated positions and become worthless. The narrower scope produces fewer resolvable claims, which is the correct trade.
9.4 The rejected shortcut
Replay was deliberately not built on the existing backtester, whose static filter check returns false outright whenever a pool-size minimum is configured or any RPC-bound filter is enabled. Under any realistic configuration it therefore answers "nothing would pass" -- a confident, precise, well-formatted, entirely artefactual answer. It is a compact illustration of the whole thesis: the function was not broken, it was answering a different question, and nothing in its return type could say so.

10. FINDING VII: IGNORANCE ACROSS A PROCESS BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Four producers, one contract
The agent runtime is domain-agnostic in a checkable sense: a source tree, a live trading system, a set of oracle feeds and a web page are all the same world type, and nothing above perception can determine which it was handed. Only the first is an in-process filesystem walk. The other three are produced by a Rust crate in a differently-pinned workspace, a stdlib-only Python package, and a browser extension with no build step -- none of which could take a dependency on the runtime without making one domain structurally privileged.
The arrangement is therefore inverted: the observed thing describes itself in the contract's vocabulary, and an importer reads it. Four rules hold on every producer. An unreadable thing is a blind spot and never a zero. Stale is not fresh and keeps its value. Every signal is a count, because a "system health score" invented in a producer would be a fabrication with a decimal point on it, laundered into a record that is cryptographically sealed and therefore looks authoritative. And a capped read reports an unknown denominator.
10.2 Enforced twice
Three of the four producers are hand-written against a JSON shape with no compiler between them and it. Each therefore restates the importer's validation on its own side and fails its own test suite; and the importer holds real captured output from all three as fixtures. A self-check catches a defect in one producer. A fixture catches the case where both sides were changed and only one of them was right, which is the failure a self-check cannot see.
The conformance checker is a single implementation shared by the importer and the operator-facing command, because two would drift and the worst available outcome is a producer that passes the checker and is then refused by the importer. It reports every finding at once, since an earlier version bailed on the first and an author with four problems learned about them one release at a time.
10.3 The closed vocabulary as a lossy channel
The largest limit on universality turned out to be a closed enumeration. With the domain and entity-kind vocabularies closed, a perceived web page and a set of price feeds both reported "unknown" -- two entirely different worlds, indistinguishable to every specialist downstream. The vocabularies were opened to known variants plus a verbatim other, held byte-for-byte so a sealed record round-trips exactly.
Parsing normalises case and padding and deliberately does not guess synonyms. An unfamiliar name is a warning and never a failure, because failing on one would push producers straight back onto "unknown" -- the collapsed encoding, re-entered voluntarily to satisfy a validator. This is a general property of strictness: a check that punishes honest specificity is a check that manufactures the vagueness it was meant to prevent.
10.4 Floats, and why the commitment is fixed-point
A commitment over raw IEEE-754 bits is unverifiable the moment a record crosses JSON, which is the only way one ever travels. The serialisation library's formatter is exact, but its parser is not correctly rounded for every seventeen-significant-digit input: a value written by one process came back one unit-in-last-place low from its own identical text. A record sealed in the daemon and re-read over HTTP therefore reported INVALID on a byte nobody had touched.
A verifier that cries tamper on an honest round trip is worse than no verifier, because the first thing anyone does with one is stop believing it -- the credibility failure of Section 4.2 and Section 5.2, now attached to a cryptographic claim. Floats are therefore encoded as fixed-point at nine decimal places, and the commitment binds values to that resolution: an edit at or above it is caught, one below it is not and cannot move any gate. The honest statement of the guarantee is narrower than "this record is unmodified", and the narrower statement is the one that is true.
10.5 The version field that must be optional
The world contract declares its schema version, and an undeclared version is refused on import, because a JSON contract implemented in four languages has no compiler standing between a producer and it, and without a version the next format change is a silent misread rather than an error. The field is optional and omitted when absent, and that is load-bearing: records sealed before the field existed must keep verifying. A verifier that retroactively condemns its own history teaches its readers to disregard it, which is the third appearance of the same credibility mechanism in this section alone.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. FINDING VIII: IGNORANCE IN NATURAL LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11.1 The last layer is prose
Every mechanism described so far terminates at a boundary it cannot cross. The final output of the conversational surface is a paragraph written by a language model, and no type carries into it. A model handed an em dash will write "0.00" if the surrounding context makes a number feel expected, and nothing downstream can detect that the substitution occurred -- which is the defect class of this dissertation, reproduced in the one medium where none of the structural defences apply.
11.2 Naming the operation
The mitigation is a prompt layer requiring every claim to be identified as one of four operations: read from a state block or tool result this turn, reasoned from something read with the derivation stateable, recalled from earlier in the conversation with the caveat that it may have moved, or guessed from general knowledge with nothing in the system having checked it. The instruction does not require labelling every sentence, which would be unusable; it requires labelling when operations are mixed in one answer, when the operator is about to act, and always when guessing.
11.3 The required output token
Phrasing determines compliance in a way that has nothing to do with meaning. An instruction to say when figures come from a simulation rather than a live system was ignored outright when written as a description of the situation; the same instruction complied with when it named a specific word the reply was required to contain. A per-turn badge in the interface remains the actual guarantee, because it is computed rather than requested -- the prompt reduces how often the text and the badge disagree, and is a mitigation rather than a mechanism.
11.4 Composition that reports itself
The prompt is a stack of declared layers rather than a string, composed under a character budget in a fixed order, and the composition reports which layers it injected. That report is returned as a response header, so a turn that went wrong is traceable to what the model was actually given. Two layers and any active gate instruction are exempt from the budget, because the failure they prevent -- a confident fabrication in the assistant's own voice -- is the one nobody can observe from outside the process.
Situational layers are placed last, beside their own data, because a rule stated next to the thing it governs survives and a rule stated in a preamble gets averaged away.
11.5 The gate that measures staleness, not mood
The assistant is gated on a coherence index computed from data integrity alone, and deliberately not from the quality of the conversation. Every read endpoint serves its state file identically whether it was written four seconds or four hours ago, and a health endpoint reports only that a process was once present. On a HOLD verdict the model is not called at all, because a warned model still writes a confident paragraph of stale numbers.
The index remains a pure function of measured data integrity by design: a run of coherent answers must not be able to talk the gate into trusting stale inputs. A system whose trust signal can be raised by its own fluent output has built a feedback loop with no external term.
11.6 What the budget experiment showed
A measured result worth reporting because it inverted the intuition. Reducing the prompt budget to fit a tighter token ceiling drops the metacognition layer first -- the layer of Section 11.2 -- because the composer ranks situational data above doctrine on purpose. The saving is roughly seven hundred tokens. The cost is the only instruction in the system that makes the model distinguish what it read from what it invented.
The general form: when a system is compressed, it discards the components with the least immediate visible value, and the components enforcing epistemic honesty have by construction no immediately visible value, because their entire function is to prevent an output that would have looked fine.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. FINDING IX: THE SAME RULE APPLIED TO CUSTODY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12.1 Three verdicts, not a boolean
An on-chain vault backs a token with a reserve asset. The obvious solvency check is whether the recorded balance equals the actual balance. It is the wrong check: anyone can transfer tokens into any account, so a surplus is normal, and it is permanently stuck, because the program moves only amounts recorded against a position and a sweeper would require a privileged role the design does not have. The check is therefore that the actual balance is at least the recorded amount, and the outcome has three states -- backed, donated, and shortfall -- of which only the last is alarming and only the last is permitted to look urgent.
A boolean would have collapsed a normal condition and a critical one into a single "not equal", producing either a false alarm on every donated vault or, after the false alarms were suppressed, no alarm at all.
12.2 Could not read is not zero
A failed read, an account owned by the wrong program, and an unconfigured program identifier are three distinct states, and none of them may render as a zero balance. "Could not read the reserve" and "the reserve is zero" are different claims, and only one of them is an accusation against the operator. This is Section 6.4 applied to money, and the consequence of getting it wrong is not a misleading chart but a public assertion of insolvency against a solvent vault.
12.3 The absent price
The page reports raw amounts and decimals and computes no valuation, because the program stores no price and consults no oracle. Adding a dollar figure or a percentage-backed number would reintroduce exactly the manipulable, arguable quantity the design removes. This is the rarest form of the discipline and the hardest to hold: declining to compute a number that everyone wants, because the inputs to it do not exist.
12.4 Decimals as a quantity of money
A token amount is converted to base units by shifting the decimal point by the mint's declared precision, so a wrong precision is a wrong quantity of money rather than a wrong label. A display table reporting six decimals for an asset whose real precision is eight would lock one hundredth of the intended reserve. Every selected mint therefore resolves its precision from the mint account itself, the amount fields stay inert until that read lands, and the route distinguishes a malformed address, a missing account, an account that is not a mint, and a failed read.
It also refuses a token account pasted in place of a mint, which is the specific trap worth recording: the two are owned by the same program, and the byte offset where precision lives in one is part of the amount field in the other, so decoding the wrong one yields a precision value read out of somebody's balance. A plausible number, correctly typed, in range, and drawn from unrelated memory.
12.5 The guarantee that is not in the source
The custody property depends on a deployment step invisible in the program's source: the upgrade authority must be revoked. Until the chain reports no authority, a program-derived vault is fully custodial regardless of how its code reads. This is stated in the deployment documentation and in this dissertation because a guarantee that cannot be verified from the artefact under review is not a guarantee, and describing it as one would be the defect class applied to trust itself.

13. THE NEGATIVE RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.1 A measurement that retired a product
An EVM chain port was built to carry the sniper to a second chain. The measured pool-creation rate on the target chain is approximately two qualifying events in eight days. A sniper is a latency product whose entire value is being early to a stream of opportunities; at two events per week there is no stream, and the product does not exist regardless of how well it is implemented.
This is recorded in the port's own documentation rather than quietly dropped, and it is the cleanest positive instance of the discipline in this dissertation. The measurement was taken, it contradicted the plan, and it was allowed to. Every mechanism described in Sections 4 through 12 exists to make this outcome possible; here it happened at the level of a roadmap rather than a float.
13.2 The unfalsified majority
Most of the machinery described here has not been tested by money. The sentience library is not wired into the trading path. The agent runtime writes nothing to the environments it observes -- its execute, delegate, discover and pay verbs are registered, documented, and exit with a diagnostic saying what is missing. The mesh is read-only by construction. The vault's custody property awaits the deployment step of Section 12.5.
The performance context remains that of the v1.13.0 window: 198 winning and 441 losing closed positions, gross profit 2.675 SOL against gross loss 0.412 SOL, a profit factor of 6.49 on a 31% win rate carried by a right-tailed winner distribution. That measurement predates every subsystem in this dissertation and says nothing about them. No claim is made that any mechanism here has improved it, and the honest summary is that the discipline has been demonstrated to prevent specific, documented, reproducible failures in development, and has not been demonstrated to improve returns.
13.3 The cost of the discipline
An agent that can decline does decline. On a barely-perceived world, most branches project exactly zero expected gain -- not by accident but because a projection may score only from signals the observer actually counted, and a dangling or estimated citation yields an absent term. The consequence is an agent that abstains from nearly everything until perception improves.
This is the risk named in Section 3.3 as a falsification condition, and it is reported as a partial confirmation rather than a refutation for one reason: the abstention is accompanied by the reason and the missing input, so the operator's next action is determined. A system that guesses on thin evidence produces an answer and no path to a better one. A system that abstains produces no answer and a specific instruction. The second is more useful only if the reason is specific, which is why Section 7.1 is load-bearing rather than a nicety -- without it, this cost would be a genuine refutation.
There is a second cost, in ceremony. Carrying a measured flag through every aggregation is verbose, it complicates arithmetic that would otherwise be one line, and Section 4.6 shows it can produce results that require a test to defend against a well-meaning simplification. The claim is not that this is free. It is that the alternative's cost is invisible and is therefore paid without being noticed.
13.4 Where the rule was got wrong first
An accounting of the occasions on which the authors of the principle violated it: the sentience index pinned at zero (4.2); the agentic gate pinned shut (4.3), eight versions and one post-mortem later; grounding inferred from keyword overlap (7.4); a coherence flag that would have defaulted off for every existing configuration (8.5); an observer that filed deliberate exclusions as blind spots (5.4); a conformance checker that reported one finding at a time (10.2); an importer that condemned records sealed before its version field existed (10.5); and a prompt budget that discarded the honesty layer first (11.6).
Eight violations, all after the principle was written down, all by parties who had read it. The pattern in every case is identical: a local decision, individually reasonable, made at a point where the alternative required more code and returned less information.

14. DISCUSSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.1 Why zero wins by default
Section 2.3 lists the mechanical reasons. The deeper one is that every layer of tooling rewards the collapsed encoding. The type checker accepts it. The serialiser produces it. The test suite, written in the same vocabulary as the interface, cannot express the distinction it would need to catch it. The renderer displays it without complaint. And the reviewer reading the diff sees a number where a number belongs.
An encoding that is invisible to the type checker, the serialiser, the tests, the renderer and the reviewer is not going to be caught by discipline alone. It is caught by making the honest encoding the one the types permit, which is why Section 5 precedes Sections 6 through 12 in dependency order even though it is the least visible piece of the work.
14.2 Ignorance is not uncertainty
A distinction that repeatedly needed restating. Uncertainty is a measured quantity: a distribution, an error bar, a confidence. Ignorance is the absence of measurement, and it has no distribution, because a distribution is itself a measurement. Systems that handle uncertainty well frequently handle ignorance badly, by assigning the unmeasured quantity a wide distribution -- which is a claim, and a strong one, asserting that the value is probably near the middle of a range somebody guessed.
The runtime's simulator converts a blind spot into measured uncertainty, which is legitimate precisely because the conversion is explicit and the blind spot was counted. The illegitimate move is the implicit one, where an unmeasured quantity acquires a prior nobody chose.
14.3 The enforcement ladder
The findings arrange themselves into a ladder of decreasing enforceability, and effort should be allocated in inverse proportion to the enforcement available. Types enforce mechanically and require no vigilance. A single shared implementation of a rule enforces by construction against everything except a deliberate second implementation. A validator enforces at a boundary, provided both sides run it and fixtures cover the case where both were changed. A test enforces only what it thought to assert. A prompt does not enforce at all and can only make compliance more likely. Prose in a specification enforces nothing and exists to explain the rungs above it.
The system's failures cluster in the middle of this ladder -- at boundaries, in renderers, and in aggregations -- which is precisely where the enforcement is partial enough to feel adequate.
14.4 The generalised principle
The v1.13.0 dissertation closed with a diagnostic rule for an investigator holding a decision log. This one closes with a construction rule for anyone building the system that writes it.
Every interface that carries a measurement must be capable of carrying its absence, and the two must remain distinguishable at every subsequent layer: through aggregation, across serialisation, through the renderer, and into whatever prose is finally written about it. Where the distinction cannot be carried, it must be reported as lost rather than resolved by substitution.
Four corollaries follow. An unmeasured dimension contributes the identity element of the operation aggregating it, and the aggregate carries the count of what was measured -- an index over two terms of nine is a statement about ignorance and must look like one. A rule that encodes a claim about trust gets exactly one implementation, and its ports are tested against it rather than against each other. A system that can abstain must abstain with a reason specific enough to determine the next action, and must not encode abstention as failure. And the components enforcing these properties will be the first discarded under any compression, because their function is to prevent an output that would have looked correct -- so they must be marked as exempt in whatever mechanism does the discarding, before that mechanism is needed.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. THREATS TO VALIDITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The corpus is a single project, and the parties who identified the defects also wrote the code containing them and are the authors of this document. There is no independent replication, no control system built without the discipline for comparison, and no blind evaluation. The claim that the discipline prevented failures rests on the failures having been observed and repaired, which is a survivorship-shaped argument: defects that the discipline failed to prevent and that were never noticed are, by construction, absent from Section 13.4.
The eight self-violations of Section 13.4 are the strongest evidence offered and they are also the most fragile, since they are self-reported and the reporting is not exhaustive. A larger count would strengthen the argument about how hard the principle is to hold; the count given is the count of violations that were caught.
No causal link is established between the discipline and any financial outcome, and Section 13.2 states plainly that none is claimed. The measured performance figures predate the work. The negative result of Section 13.1 is a genuine measurement of an external system and is the least project-dependent evidence here, but it is one measurement over eight days on one chain.
Finally, the arithmetic claims of Section 4 are demonstrated by two incidents rather than by analysis of a population of systems. The claim that multiplicative aggregation is uniquely hazardous to the collapsed encoding is a claim about a form, and two instances of a form failing in one project is an anecdote with a mechanism attached, not a frequency.

16. FUTURE WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Four directions follow directly. The sentience index remains outside the trading path; wiring it in would put the discipline where money can falsify it, which is the only test that has not been run. The action verbs of the agent runtime are unimplemented pending an approval model with risk declared per operation and a spending policy, and the sequencing there is deliberate -- a system that can act should acquire the ability to decline before it acquires the ability to spend.
The commitment scheme is tamper-evident and not tamper-proof, since the root is not anchored anywhere outside the author's control. Anchoring it is a bounded piece of work that would convert a claim about editing into a claim about originality.
And the perception layer reads file contents to count markers with no equivalent of the protected-pattern refusal used elsewhere in the project. It emits counts and never contents, which bounds the exposure, but the correct posture is a refusal before the read rather than a bound on the output.

17. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The earlier dissertation on this system found four dead signals and concluded that a gate consuming a constant is a constant function wearing the costume of a predicate. Fourteen versions later the more general statement is available: a system that cannot represent its own ignorance will manufacture the observations it lacks, and it will manufacture them in the shape that its types, its serialiser, its tests and its renderer all accept without comment.
The defence is not cleverness and it is not care. It is a representation in which the honest answer is expressible, defended at each layer by the strongest enforcement that layer permits, and an accounting -- kept honestly, including the eight occasions on which its own authors got it wrong -- of where the representation was lost anyway.
What such a system buys is not accuracy. It is the ability to distinguish its own reliable outputs from its unreliable ones, which is a smaller property and the only one that holds up on the day it matters. A trading system that halts when its safety checks stop resolving has not become profitable; it has become capable of noticing that it is flying blind, and of saying so, in a form specific enough that somebody can go and fix it. That is the whole claim, and after fourteen versions of building against it, it appears to be worth what it costs.
