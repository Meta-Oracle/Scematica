# Scematica Roadmap
## Q1 2026 → Q1 2027

---

# Vision

Scematica is being built as a next-generation autonomous AI trading infrastructure powered by open-source intelligence, predictive market systems, and scalable agentic integrations.

The mission is not to create another hype-based trading bot.
The mission is to engineer a fully reactive, prediction-driven autonomous trading architecture capable of adapting to real-time market conditions across Web3 ecosystems.

Built with integrations around Claude, x402, Dexter, Eliza, and future agentic frameworks, Scematica aims to become the foundation layer for intelligent decentralized trading systems.
