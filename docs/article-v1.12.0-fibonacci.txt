Scematica v1.12.0 — Fibonacci Protocol Live

The Fibonacci recovery system is now fully wired into the sniper execution path. This is not a config toggle or a paper spec. It is running on every pool event, every price tick, every exit decision.

Here is what changed.


Entry Gate

Every incoming pool now passes through FibonacciRecoverySystem before it reaches the pool scorer. The gate evaluates four signals against golden-ratio thresholds:

  Pool size — 35% weight. Sweet spot is 8 to 21 SOL, the F6 to F8 Fibonacci band.
  Pool age — 30% weight. Peak score at 3 seconds or under. Acceptable up to 13 seconds.
  Inflow velocity — 25% weight. Strong at 1.618 SOL/s (phi). Exceptional at 2.618 SOL/s (phi squared).
  Buy pressure — 10% weight. Golden ratio confirmation when quote/base ratio exceeds 1.618.

Pools scoring below 0.55 on the composite signal are rejected with a logged reason before any RPC call is made for scoring. The gate is cheap. The rejection is immediate.

The Fibonacci Runner fast-lane is a separate path. When all four signals hit maximum strength simultaneously — 8 to 21 SOL, under 5 seconds, velocity above 2.618 SOL/s, ratio above 1.618 — the pool bypasses normal scheduling and executes immediately. No queue. No wait.


Position Sizing

The position multiplier is now driven by the entry score:

  Score 0.90 or above — 2.0x. Exceptional setup.
  Score 0.75 to 0.89 — 1.618x. The golden ratio applied directly to size.
  Score 0.55 to 0.74 — 1.0x. Baseline.
  Below 0.55 — rejected at the gate. Never reaches sizing.

This means the bot automatically concentrates capital on the highest-conviction entries without any manual adjustment. The math does the work.


Exit Tracking

FibonacciMomentum runs inside every sell monitor task. The golden retracement exit fires when the position pulls back 61.8% from its peak while in profit. This sits alongside the existing exit ladder — trailing stop, velocity decay, adaptive pullback, tiered partial TP — as a first-class exit signal.

Stats are logged every 10 exits: win rate, average PnL, entry count, exit count. The fib_system and fib_stats are shared via Arc across the sniper and all concurrent sell monitor tasks, consistent with the file-based IPC architecture the rest of the system uses.
